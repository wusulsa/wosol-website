Employee transport in Jeddah

Staff transport for companies, institutions and authorities in Jeddah, connecting accommodation and pickup points with the workplace. Request a monthly or annual arrangement based on passenger numbers, shifts and routes.

Plan journeys between accommodation and workplaces

For a Jeddah business with offices, branches or teams working different hours, each group needs its own pickup assessment. Accurate locations and arrival times help compare a shared route with several shorter routes, rather than assuming one long collection run suits everyone.

Choose capacity around the journey

Fleet categories include 4-, 5- or 7-passenger vehicles, 9- or 11-passenger minibuses, 21-passenger mid-size buses and 51-passenger coaches. Passenger numbers and route access guide the choice of vehicle.

What affects the quotation?

Route length, number of pickup points, trips per day, shifts, operating days, vehicle capacity and contract duration all affect pricing. Include waiting time and any special site requirements so the scope is clear before agreement.

Prepare a request your team can review

Send your organisation name, contact person, location links, passengers per journey, start and finish times, operating days and proposed contract dates. Commercial registration, tax certificate and national address documents are optional at the request stage.

Agree the operating plan before starting

Define the approved timetable, coordinator and change process. Agree how delays are reported and what replacement arrangements apply, including any limitations. Tracking and operational reporting are included in the service.

Frequently asked questions

Can employees be collected from different Jeddah neighbourhoods?

You can request multiple pickup points. Their sequence, journey time and capacity are reviewed before the route and price are agreed.

Should we use one pickup point or several?

Start with employee accommodation locations and required arrival times. One shared pickup point reduces stops; several points may suit groups living farther apart. Passenger distribution and routing guide the arrangement; there is no single journey time for every neighbourhood.

How can we compare staff transport quotes?

Compare the same route, trip frequency, operating days, capacity and contract duration. Review price inclusions, waiting time, schedule changes and replacement arrangements. Price alone does not explain differences in service scope.
