Service areas

We welcome requests from Jeddah, Riyadh, Makkah, Madinah, Dammam and other regions across Saudi Arabia. Share your locations, schedule and requirements to help us plan the service around your needs.

Plan stops and waiting time

Tell us whether the vehicle stays with the group or returns at a set time. Include stops and the expected return time so the quote covers the requested route, hire period and waiting arrangements.

A vehicle suited to your group

We match the vehicle category to passenger numbers, luggage and route conditions. Schools and centres should identify their group coordinator and boarding and drop-off requirements.

Service coverage across Saudi Arabia

Our service focuses on Jeddah and Riyadh, with requests welcomed for Makkah, Madinah, Dammam, AlUla and other Saudi regions.
