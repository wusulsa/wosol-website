HELPFUL ANSWERS

Your questions.
Clearer answers.

Are all university campuses covered?

Al Sulaymaniyah is the campus currently listed in the form. Contact us to confirm availability for any other campus before registering.

Does submitting the form reserve a seat?

Submission is a subscription request. Our team confirms coverage, availability and details before the service is agreed.

When should I enquire for a new term or exams?

Enquire when your timetable is available. Share your neighbourhood, campus, attendance days and outbound and return times. Send exam timetable changes to the team for route review. Seats and schedule changes require confirmation.

How much does employee transport in Jeddah cost?

Cost depends on routes, passenger count, vehicle requirements, shifts, operating days and contract duration. Share your requirements for a suitable quote.

How do I plan seasonal shifts or a new site?

Share start and end dates, passengers per shift and pickup and destination links. Coverage, capacity and schedules are reviewed before a quote is confirmed. An enquiry is not a confirmed booking.
